How to Play (Game Mechanics)

1. Pass & Peek: Hand the phone to each registered player. Non-impostors see the Secret Word (e.g. Knife). The secret Impostor only gets a category clue (e.g. Kitchen Item).

2. Give Clues: Players take turns saying a single word or short clue describing the Secret Word. The Impostor must fake it and blend in!

3. Catch the Impostor: Spot whoever gives suspicious or off-target clues, discuss with the crew, and tap Unmask to reveal the results!
